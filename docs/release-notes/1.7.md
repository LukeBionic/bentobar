### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Optional online items.** Only **Weather** and **Air quality & UV** (Open-Meteo, no key), **Exchange rates** (Frankfurter, no key), **Flight** (AirLabs, with a free key of your own) and **US stocks** (Finnhub, with a free key of your own) go online, and only after you set them up; **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/Meridian11Labs/bentobar#readme).
